- `pinv_spec`: **FAITHFUL**. It gives `A * pinv A * A = A`, `pinv A * A * pinv A = pinv A` and the symmetry of `A * pinv A` and `pinv A * A`, which are the row's "four equations".
- `pinv_unique`: **FAITHFUL**. It gives the row's "uniqueness": every `X` that satisfies the four equations is `pinv A`.
- `pinv_residual_dotProduct_le`: **FAITHFUL**. It covers the row's "least squares with a rank-deficient design". For every `A`, with no rank hypothesis, `pinv A *ᵥ y` has a residual sum of squares at most that of every `β`, so rank-deficient designs are included.
- `pinv_eq_leastSquares`: **FAITHFUL**. It adds to the row's least-squares part. When `(Aᵀ * A).det` is a unit, which over `ℝ` means full column rank, `pinv A` is the library's `ClassicalLean.Statistics.leastSquares A = (Aᵀ * A)⁻¹ * Aᵀ` (definition read in `Statistics/GaussMarkov.lean`, line 116).
