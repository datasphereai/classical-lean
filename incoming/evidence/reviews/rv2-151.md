## The main theorem against its plan row

- `hasLaw_sum_sq_gaussian`: **FAITHFUL**. For `k ≥ 1` measurable, mutually independent (`iIndepFun`) real random variables on a probability space, each with law `gaussianReal 0 1` (mean 0, variance 1), the sum of squares has law `gammaMeasure ((k : ℝ) / 2) (1 / 2)`. In Mathlib that law has density `r ^ a / Gamma a * x ^ (a - 1) * exp (-(r * x))` on `x ≥ 0` (`gammaPDFReal`, read in Mathlib's source), so its shape is `k/2` and its rate is `1/2`, as the row says. The two hypotheses the row does not name are `0 < k`, which the row's `k` degrees of freedom presuppose (at `k = 0` the claim is false: the sum is the constant 0, while `gammaMeasure 0 (1/2)` is the zero measure, because Mathlib's `Gamma 0` is 0), and measurability, which is what a random variable is.
