## Main theorem against its row

the order's table.

The row assumes a convex `f` with subgradients of norm at most `G` and step `η`. It concludes that
the best of the first `T` points is within `(‖x₀ − z‖² + η² G² T)/(2 η T)` of `f z`. The theorem
has the row's conclusion exactly:
`(range T).inf' _ (fun n => f (x n)) - f z ≤ (‖x 0 - z‖ ^ 2 + η ^ 2 * G ^ 2 * T) / (2 * η * T)`.
That is the best of `x 0, …, x (T - 1)`, and "within" is read one-sided, as is standard. The
theorem asks no convexity. It asks only the subgradient inequality `hsub` at the iterates, and
every subgradient of a convex `f` satisfies it. So the row's statement follows.

The hypotheses:

* `hsub` (subgradient at `x n`), `hg` (`‖g n‖ ≤ G`) and `hstep` (`x (n + 1) = x n - η • g n`)
  are the row's.
* `hη : 0 < η` is the row's step size, and the denominator needs it.
* `hT : 0 < T` is regularity. It makes "the first `T` points" nonempty, which `Finset.inf'`
  needs. It is used in the statement itself.

None of them is weaker than the row, and each one is used: the five `clear` fire tests above all
fail. In the proof, `hη` is used at lines 73 and 93, `hT` at lines 55, 84 and 92, `hsub` at line
68, `hg` at line 66 and `hstep` at line 61.
