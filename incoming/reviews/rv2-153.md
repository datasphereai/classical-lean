## Each main theorem against its plan row

- `huffman_optimal`: **GENERALIZED**. Over a two-letter alphabet it gives an injective prefix-free code with Huffman's lengths whose mean length is at most that of every injective prefix-free code, which is the row's claim, and it allows any nonnegative weights, not only a probability vector (injectivity of the competitors is what makes them codes: without it every symbol could get the word `[]`).
- `huffmanLength` (the definition): **FAITHFUL**. It is binary Huffman: while two or more symbols are left, merge a lightest and a next-lightest (`pick`) into one of their summed weight (`mergeW`), recurse on one symbol fewer, and give both merged symbols the merged symbol's length plus one (`mergeLen`); `Fintype.card ι` steps suffice, since the set shrinks by one per step.
