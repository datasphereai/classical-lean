`P(X i ≤ x) = F` and gives the chance that the `k`-th smallest is at most `x` as

## Private helpers

Each one is true, carries no hidden assumption, and gives exactly what its use needs:
* `orderStat_le_iff`: `orderStat v k ≤ x ↔ k + 1 ≤ #{i | v i ≤ x}`.
  * `⇒`: the first `k + 1` sorted entries are at most `x`.
  * `⇐`: if the entry at sorted position `k` is above `x`, so is every later one, and at most `k`
    entries are at most `x`.
  * It holds with ties. It is used in `hev` (line 137).
* `prod_ite_mem_eq`: `∏ i : Fin n, (if i ∈ S then a else b) = a ^ #S * b ^ (n - #S)`, true for every
  `S`, `a` and `b`. It is used in `hpiece` (line 202).
* `sum_filter_card`: the sum over subsets `S` of `Fin n` with `k ≤ #S` of `a ^ #S * b ^ (n - #S)`
  equals `∑ j ∈ Icc k n, C(n, j) a ^ j b ^ (n - j)`. There are `C(n, j)` subsets of size `j`. It is
  true for every `k`, both sides being `0` when `k > n`. It is used at line 206.
