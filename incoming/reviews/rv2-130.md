## Main theorems against row 130

The row reads: "The binary symmetric channel with crossover `ε` has capacity `log 2 − h(ε)`,
reached by the uniform input."

* **`mutualInfo_bsc_le` (line 130): FAITHFUL.**
  * What it states: take `ε ∈ [0, 1]` and any probability vector `p` on `Fin 2`. The library's
    mutual information of the joint law `p a * (if a = b then 1 - ε else ε)` is at most
    `Real.log 2 - Real.binEntropy ε`. That is the bound half of "has capacity `log 2 − h(ε)`".
  * Its hypotheses are all the row's. `hε0` and `hε1` make `ε` a crossover probability, which is
    what the row's word means. `hp0` and `hp1` make `p` an input law, and the capacity is a
    supremum over input laws. None is extra.
  * `hε0`, `hε1` and `hp0` go to `bscJoint_nonneg` (lines 136 and 147). That gives `hP0`, which
    `hY0` and `mutualInfo_eq_…` need. The fire test shows that `bscJoint_nonneg` needs both bounds
    on `ε`.
  * `hp1` gives `hY1`: the output law sums to 1, which `shannonEntropy_le_log_card` needs. It is
    also used in `mutualInfo_bscJoint` (`h1`).
  * `hp1` cannot be dropped. At `ε = 1/2` and `p = (1/e, 0)` the mutual information is
    `-(1/e) log (1/e) = 1/e`, which is more than `log 2 - h(1/2) = 0`.
* **`mutualInfo_bsc_half` (line 152): FAITHFUL.**
  * What it states: at the uniform input `1 / 2`, the mutual information equals
    `log 2 − h(ε)` for every `ε ∈ [0, 1]`. That is "reached by the uniform input".
  * With the first theorem: the supremum over input laws is `log 2 − h(ε)`, and it is attained.
  * Its hypotheses `hε0` and `hε1` are the row's (a crossover probability). They are used: they
    go to `mutualInfo_bscJoint` and then to `bscJoint_nonneg`, which needs each one (fire test).
  * The library has no `capacity` definition. The pair (a bound for every law, and equality at the
    uniform law) is the row's statement. Job 1 gave the same verdict.
